Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Haiku Messenger ("Haiku", "we", "our") is designed from the ground up for private, ephemeral, and quiet conversation. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in via Google OAuth, we collect your verified email address, chosen display name, username, and declared date of birth (to verify the 13+ age requirement).
  • Messages and Content: Messages sent on Haiku are ephemeral and automatically expire based on conversation timers. Voice notes and media expire after their configured duration (e.g. 10 minutes) and are permanently purged from servers.
  • Google User Data: When signing in with Google, we only access basic profile information (email and name) to verify your identity. We do not access Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or any other Google services.

2. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate your session and protect your account.
  • To transmit ephemeral messages and media between you and your recipients.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements or perform algorithmic behavioral tracking.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Expired messages, media, and temporary voice notes are purged automatically from our databases and storage buckets. You may request account deletion at any time from the app settings, which initiates permanent removal of all associated account data.
