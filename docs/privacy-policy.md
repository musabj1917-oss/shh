# Haiku Messenger — Privacy Policy & Disclosure Draft

## Overview
Haiku Messenger is committed to protecting user privacy, particularly young users aged 13+.

## Key Disclosures

1. **Server-Side Messaging & Moderation**:
   - Haiku Messenger uses standard server-side message verification and database storage, NOT end-to-end encryption.
   - The authorized platform owner/admin can review active unexpired messages, user profiles, groups, and submitted safety reports for moderation and safety compliance.

2. **Automated Content Expiry**:
   - **Photos, Videos, and Voice Notes**: Automatically expire 10 minutes after sending and are purged from database records and storage.
   - **Stories**: Expire after 24 hours.
   - **Expired Content**: Once expired, content is permanently deleted from application storage and is inaccessible to all users and admins.

3. **Public Profiles & Birthday Visibility**:
   - For COPPA and child safety compliance, birth years are NEVER displayed publicly or in API responses.
   - Friends can only view the user's birth month and day (e.g., "May 15").

4. **Account Deletion Lifecycle**:
   - Users can request account deletion at any time in Profile Settings.
   - Accounts are scheduled for deletion with a 30-day cancellation grace period.
   - Permanent deletion purges user profile details, sessions, media assets, friendships, and decoy credentials.

5. **Limits of Deletion**:
   - Deletion cannot prevent recipients from taking screenshots, external device recordings, or saving content locally on personal devices.
